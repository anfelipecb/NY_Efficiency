## How they fit together

- **MMR** is the measurement system: what agencies spend and what they deliver.
- **EO 12** is the savings system: each agency looks inward at cost drivers, coordinated by the First Deputy Mayor and OMB.
- **COGE** is the rules system: fix Charter-level process so every future permit, contract, and project moves faster.
- **EO 13** is a coordination example: the First Deputy Mayor's office running a deadline-driven, cross-agency audit, and a reminder that trust affects whether services reach people.

Put simply: the MMR tells you where to look, EO 12 and COGE are the two levers (agency budgets and citywide rules), and the Reach pane is a check that savings do not come out of what residents actually receive.
